            Three strands, three standings

            Our choice

            The picture

            Higher dimensions are best shown by what they leave behind in a lower world: shadows, slices, a field rising from a plane.

            Established physics

            The physics

            A lower-dimensional boundary encodes the volume (’t Hooft 1993; Susskind 1995; Maldacena 1998), and an evaporating hole hands its information back (Page 1993).

            Our choice

            The framing

            The source plane as a flatland of metaphysical infinity. Physics permits that reading and does not prove it.

            The research: nine versions, three acts

            Act I

            The medium

            Spacetime drawn as a resonating superfluid over a flat source plane, with magnetism entering only as quantized flux tubes.

            Act II

            The horizon

            The fluid drains into a spinning vortex, and a horizon forms where the flow outruns the wave speed (Unruh 1981; Visser 1998).

            Act III

            The information

            The hole becomes a 13-qubit register that evaporates into the source plane and gives its message back.

            What the model does not show

            That spacetime is a superfluid

            The fluid picture and the qubit engine are joined by our own mapping. Neither is derived from the other.

            How gravity makes islands

            The island formula is an input, and the circuit cut is an analogy to it. Replica wormholes are not derived.

            A hole of realistic size

            Thirteen qubits is tiny. Curves are noisy, and the turn lands near six or seven qubits.

            A new prediction

            Every number reproduces a published result. Nothing yet separates this picture from standard physics.

            Where the thesis stands Our choice · assessment

            Reproduced

            One seeded engine reproduces the Page curve, the island rule, the mirror effect and a working decoder.

            Argued

            A flat source plane works as the picture of a boundary that holds the information. That is a design argument.

            Not established

            That spacetime is a superfluid, or that the plane is a metaphysical infinity. The model cannot test either.

            A faithful, sourced demonstration of established physics. Not yet a theory of its own.
